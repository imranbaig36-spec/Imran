## Ticket L-05 — Contract Specialist (generic answers on contract templates library)

**Subject:** Copilot Responses for Contract Templates — Investigation

Hi,

Thank you for the detail you have provided. When Copilot gives generic answers without referencing the specific documents you are asking about, it usually means it is not successfully grounding on that content. This can happen for a few reasons: the library content may not yet be fully indexed, access permissions on the library may be preventing grounding, or document protection settings may be blocking it.

**What we are checking:**
- Whether the contract templates library is fully indexed in Microsoft Search.
- Whether any permissions or sensitivity labels on the library are preventing Copilot from reading document content.
- Whether your account has the full set of entitlements needed for SharePoint grounding.

**A quick test you can try now:**
1. Open one of the contract templates directly in SharePoint or Word Online.
2. With the document open, ask Copilot: *"Summarise this document"* or *"What does clause 3 say?"*
3. If Copilot still gives a generic answer, please send us a screenshot — this will help us confirm the issue quickly.

We will follow up with findings and a resolution path as soon as our checks are complete.
