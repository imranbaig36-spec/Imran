# L1 Self-Service: Copilot Showed a Legal Matter You Did Not Expect
Version: 1.0
Last Updated: 2026-08-14
Derived From: Legal Floor 6 Copilot Access/Permissions Remediation Runbook v1.0

Runbook Traceability (single source assurance):
- This article is a user-facing re-expression of the same source runbook, not an independent process.
- Runbook Step 1-2 -> L1 Steps 1-2 (classify, preserve evidence).
- Runbook Step 3-6 -> Service Desk/Security actions (described under "What happens next").
- Runbook Step 7-9 -> L1 Step 4 and closure expectation (wait for controlled verification).

If Copilot shows a client matter you do not expect to see, treat it as urgent and report it right away.

1. Stop and do not continue asking Copilot about that matter.
Expected result: No additional exposure risk from continued queries.

2. Capture what happened.
Action: Save time, screenshot, your prompt, and what Copilot returned.
Expected result: Security gets enough evidence to investigate quickly.

3. Report to Service Desk immediately as a security/confidentiality incident.
Action: Include your name, device, time, and evidence.
Expected result: Incident is escalated to Security/Data-Governance.

4. Wait for Security guidance before retrying.
Expected result: Fix and verification happen in a controlled process.

What happens next: Security validates permissions, applies a targeted fix, verifies the issue no longer reproduces, and confirms closure with evidence.

Do not share screenshots or incident details broadly; send only through approved support channels.
