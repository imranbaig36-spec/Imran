# Leadership Update — Win 11 Migration: Fin Bridge Staff
**Date:** 2026-08-12
**Prepared by:** DWP Endpoint Team
**Audience:** Non-technical management

---

The Windows 11 migration for Fin Bridge staff has largely landed well. The majority of colleagues reported no significant disruption, and a number described the new devices as noticeably faster and more responsive than before. Issues that emerged in the first week — including staff being locked out of their accounts, VPN connections dropping during client calls, and missing files in OneDrive — have all been resolved and are no longer generating complaints. Login speeds have returned to normal, and the positive tone in staff feedback has increased week on week. Overall, this represents a successful migration for the bulk of the 215 affected users.

Two issues remain open and are being actively worked. The floor 3 printer has not automatically connected since migration day and the team there is still walking to another floor to print — this is now entering its third week and has been escalated as a priority fix. Separately, the finance team has begun reporting that Excel is freezing and crashing when working on large files, with some colleagues losing unsaved work as a result. This appears to be linked to a security configuration change applied to finance devices last week, and the team is currently investigating which part of that change is causing the slowdown, with a view to adjusting the setting without compromising the security requirement. Both issues are being tracked and owners have been assigned. A further update on both will be provided by end of week.
