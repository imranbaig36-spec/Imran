## New Theme in Week 2 (Not Present in Week 1)

---

### NEW — Excel Crashing on Large Files
**Week 2 count:** 9 comments | Severity: **Blocker** (for finance users)
W2 IDs: 4, 8, 13, 16, 20, 24, 30, 33, 37

This theme did not appear at all in week 1. It has emerged as the dominant new complaint, specifically affecting the finance team. Crashes occur on large files (one user specifies files over 10MB), users are losing unsaved work, and multiple comments describe it as their single biggest current issue. The finance team is the most affected group, which makes this high business risk given month-end cycles.

> *"Excel crash is really disruptive, losing unsaved work each time."* (W2, id 13)
> *"Excel crashing has become a real productivity problem for finance team."* (W2, id 20)
> *"New Excel crash issue -- happens specifically with files over 10MB."* (W2, id 33)

**Recommended next action:** Investigate whether the Win 11 migration changed Office channel, memory allocation, or add-in configuration for finance devices. This should be treated as a Blocker for the finance team and escalated immediately.
