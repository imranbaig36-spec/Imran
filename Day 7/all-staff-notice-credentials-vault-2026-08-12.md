# All-Staff Notice — Credentials Vault Unavailable
**Date:** 2026-08-12
**From:** IT Service Desk
**To:** Fin Bridge All Staff

---

We are aware that the shared credentials vault is currently inaccessible for a number of colleagues following the Windows 11 migration. We want you to know this is a known issue — you have not done anything wrong, and we are actively working to restore access.

**While we investigate, please try the following:**

1. Close and reopen your browser, then attempt to access the vault again — occasional session errors can prevent the page from loading correctly.
2. If you urgently need a specific credential to continue working, contact the Service Desk directly. We will provide that credential securely through an alternative method so you are not blocked while the vault is being restored.

Please do not attempt to store or share credentials through email or chat as a workaround — this is a security risk and is not necessary while we have an alternative process in place.

**We will provide a progress update by 14:00 today.** We are not yet in a position to give a fix time, but we are treating this as a priority incident.

Thank you for your patience — we know this is disruptive and we appreciate you raising it with us.
