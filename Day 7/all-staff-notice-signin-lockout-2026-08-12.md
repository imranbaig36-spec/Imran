# All-Staff Notice — Sign-In and Account Lockout Issues
**Date:** 2026-08-12
**From:** IT Service Desk
**To:** Fin Bridge All Staff

---

We are aware that a number of colleagues are experiencing difficulties signing in this morning, including accounts becoming locked after the recent Windows 11 migration. We are actively investigating and working to resolve this as quickly as possible.

**While we work on a fix, please try the following:**

1. If your account appears locked, wait 15 minutes and try again — it may unlock automatically.
2. Check your Microsoft Authenticator app for any sign-in approval notifications you may have missed.
3. Try resetting your own password using the Self-Service Password Reset tool at **aka.ms/sspr** — you can do this from any device including your phone, without needing to call the Service Desk.

If you are still unable to log in after trying the above, please contact the Service Desk and let us know your name, staff ID, the error message on screen, and when the problem first started. This helps us resolve your case faster.

**We will send an update by 12:00 today.**

Thank you for your patience — we know this is disruptive and we are treating it as a priority.

*IT Service Desk*
