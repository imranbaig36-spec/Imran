# Win 11 Migration — Self-Service Guides for Common Issues
**Date:** 2026-08-12
**Audience:** Fin Bridge Staff
**Plain language guides — no IT knowledge needed**

---

## 1. Can't Log In / Account Locked Out

**Try this first:**
Wait 15 minutes and try logging in again — your account may unlock itself automatically after a short time. When you try again, make sure Caps Lock is off and type your password carefully.

If you use an app on your phone to approve your sign-in (like Microsoft Authenticator), open the app and check for a waiting approval notification you may have missed.

**Still locked out? Contact the Service Desk and tell them:**
- Your full name and staff ID
- The exact error message you see on screen
- What time it first happened and how many times it has occurred

---

## 2. Floor 3 Printer Not Appearing

**Try this first:**
Sign out of your computer and sign back in — this can trigger the printer to reconnect automatically. Once you are back on your desktop, wait two minutes before trying to print.

If the printer still does not appear, try restarting the printer itself using the power button on the device.

**Still not working? Contact the Service Desk and tell them:**
- That you are on Floor 3 and the printer has not appeared since the Windows 11 migration
- Your computer name (shown at the bottom of the login screen or you can ask a colleague to find it)
- Whether anyone else nearby has the same problem

---

## 3. VPN Keeps Disconnecting

**Try this first:**
Disconnect from the VPN completely, wait 30 seconds, then reconnect. If you are using home Wi-Fi, try moving closer to your router or switching to a wired connection if you have one available.

Avoid running large downloads or video streams at the same time as a VPN call — these can cause the connection to drop.

**Still dropping? Contact the Service Desk and tell them:**
- Roughly how often the VPN drops (e.g. every 10 minutes)
- Whether it happens on calls specifically or all the time
- Your location (home, office, or other) and the type of connection you are using (Wi-Fi or cable)
