# Title: Black Screen After Sign-In to Your Virtual Desktop
# Version: 1.0
# Date: 07/08/2026
# status: Draft

If you sign in and only see a black screen, your files are safe. This issue does not delete your documents or change saved work.

## What You Can Do

1. Wait for 30 seconds.
   Sometimes the screen opens by itself after a short delay.

2. If nothing changes, sign out of the work screen.
   Use the sign-out option in the app you used to connect, or close the window if you cannot reach your normal work screen.

3. Wait 1 minute.
   This gives the system time to reset your connection.

4. Sign in again.
   Many users can get in normally on the next attempt.

5. If the screen is still black, try signing in one more time.
   Do not keep trying again and again.

6. If the problem continues after two more tries, contact the Service Desk.
   Let them know you can sign in, but the desktop stays black or does not fully load.

## Before You Contact The Service Desk

Please include these details in your request:

- Your work username
- The time of your latest failed sign-in attempt
- Whether the screen stayed black or loaded after a delay
- Whether you were signed out or had to close the window yourself
- The device you used, such as a company laptop, home PC, or work terminal
- Whether you used the app or a web browser
- A screenshot, if possible

## Contact The Service Desk If...

Contact the Service Desk if the screen stays black after two more tries, if you keep getting signed out, or if you still cannot get to your normal work screen after waiting and signing in again.
